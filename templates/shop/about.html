About Dabi Jutti House

Dabi Jutti House is a Jaipur-based footwear brand celebrating the rich heritage of Rajasthani craftsmanship. For over two decades, our artisans have been handcrafting juttis, mojaris, and ethnic footwear using traditional techniques passed down through generations.

We serve both retail customers looking for a single special pair and wholesale buyers who need bulk supply for their shops, boutiques, wedding events, and corporate gifting.

Our Collections

  • Rajasthani Juttis with zari, mirror & thread embroidery
  • Punjabi Juttis with phulkari work
  • Traditional Mojaris from Jodhpur & Jaipur
  • Authentic Kolhapuri Chappals
  • Bridal & Wedding special footwear
  • Kids ethnic & casual footwear
